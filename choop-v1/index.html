THE GOOD-TO-KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

Curious about CHOOP? Here’s a little more about the idea.

What is CHOOP?+

CHOOP is a proposed social coupon platform for discovering, sharing, and redeeming offers.

How do I claim a coupon?+

Claiming is part of the proposed experience. The details are still being developed.

Can I share coupons with friends?+

Sharing offers with friends is central to the CHOOP concept. The exact features are still being defined.

How do coupon redemptions work?+

Redemption rules will depend on each offer and are yet to be finalized.

Is CHOOP free to use?+

The coupon business model has not yet been finalized.
